## Narration (English take, stand-ins in *italics*)

1. HOOK — Not every kanji came from China. Japan created some of its own!
2. 国字 — They're called *kokuji*, Japanese-created kanji. And some of them are hiding in plain sight!
3. 働 — First, look at this character: *ugoku*. It means to move. Now, add *hito*, a person. Combine them,
   and you get *hataraku*, meaning to work!
4. 峠 — Next: mountain, up, and down. Together they make *touge*, a mountain pass, a path through the mountains.
5. 畑 — And fire, plus rice field, makes *hatake*, a cultivated field.
6. MORE — Some compiled lists contain over two hundred of these characters. Here are a few more!
   (cards: 凪 なぎ calm sea · 雫 しずく drop · 辻 つじ crossroads · 枠 わく frame · 匂 におい scent ·
   笹 ささ bamboo grass · 榊 さかき sakaki tree · 鰯 いわし sardine)
7. DREAM — Every kanji has a story. Every mistake helps you grow. Keep learning, one step at a time.
   Your Japanese journey is still being written!
   Japanese line: 一歩ずつ、夢に近づこう。 (Ippo zutsu, yume ni chikazukō.)
8. FINAL — (separate take) Thanks for watching till the end! Follow me for more.
