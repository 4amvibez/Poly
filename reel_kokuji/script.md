# Reel 6 — JAPAN CREATED ITS OWN KANJI?! (@raj_in_jp)

English: Raj (3HL8elsjVw0CPWZOXkOf).  Japanese: Raj in Japanese (O9wrU9R3lqrtkwAuQhDV).
Every Japanese word is spoken in the Japanese voice. Romaji stand-ins in the English take are replaced by
build_vo.py with clips from the Japanese list take.

## 1. HOOK (0:00–0:03)

Visual: 亻 and 動 slam onto the paper, far apart, with a sharp sound. WHAT MAKES THIS? appears.
The two parts lock together into 働, and MADE IN JAPAN is stamped on it.

Voice (Raj): "Two parts. Not China. Japan made this."

## 2. KOKUJI (0:03–0:09)

Visual: 国字 appears, with こくじ above it and JAPANESE-CREATED KANJI below.

Voice (Raj): "They're called kokuji, Japanese-created kanji. And some of them are hiding in plain sight!"

## 3. 働 (0:09–0:22)

Visual: 動 (to move) is pushed by 人 (a person). The two lock together into 働 (to work).

Voice (Raj): "This part is ugoku, to move. Add hito, a person, who pushes it. Put them together, and you get
hataraku, to work. A person moving something: that's work!"

On-screen: 動 うごく TO MOVE → 人 ひと PERSON → 働 はたらく TO WORK

## 4. 峠 (0:22–0:30)

Visual: 山 + 上 + 下 make a mountain pass.

Voice (Raj): "Mountain, up, and down together make touge, a mountain pass."

## 5. 畑 (0:30–0:37)

Visual: 火 + 田 make a cultivated field.

Voice (Raj): "Fire, plus rice field, makes hatake, a cultivated field."

## 6. MORE KOKUJI (0:37–0:45)

Visual: eight cards, laid down one by one, with a pause so people can read them: 凪 雫 辻 枠 匂 笹 榊 鰯.

Voice (Raj): "Dictionaries list hundreds of kokuji. Here are a few more!"

## 7. FINAL (0:45–0:52)

Visual: the red sun sinks into the paper. THANK YOU FOR WATCHING TILL THE END! and FOLLOW @raj_in_jp appear.

Voice (Raj): "Thanks for watching, till the end, and follow for more."
Japanese (Raj in Japanese): またね！ (matane)

- The 働 explanation (push, then work) is a visual memory aid, not the full history of the character.
- 働 also has an on'yomi (ドウ, as in 労働). The reel teaches はたらく only.
- Recorded: audio/take_en_c.mp3 (Raj, Scribe exact), Japanese words from audio/words_ja_a.mp3 and またね from
  audio/matane_a.mp3 (Raj in Japanese). The spliced narration was Scribe-checked: every word, tōge with its long o.
- Music: the user's track (audio/bgm.mp3). Its own opening runs under the reel, then a beat-matched crossfade goes into
  its real ending, so the last bar lands with the reel. It is ducked under every word: the voice leads the music and
  effects by a median of 24 dB while Raj speaks.
