# Reel 6 — JAPAN CREATED ITS OWN KANJI?! (@raj_in_jp)

English: Raj (3HL8elsjVw0CPWZOXkOf).  Japanese: Raj in Japanese (O9wrU9R3lqrtkwAuQhDV).
Every Japanese word is spoken in the Japanese voice. Romaji stand-ins in the English take are replaced by

The hook is the first 3 seconds and must pay off by 2.5 s. Everything after it explains the puzzle it sets up.

## 1. HOOK (0:00–0:03)

Visual: 亻 and 動 slam onto the paper, far apart, with a sharp sound. At 0.4 s, WHAT MAKES THIS? appears.
At 1.0 s the two parts slide together with a thud and lock into 働. At 1.5 s a red stamp hits: MADE IN JAPAN.

Voice (Raj): "Two parts. Not China. Japan made this."

## 2. KOKUJI (0:03–0:09)

Visual: 国字 with こくじ above it, JAPANESE-CREATED KANJI below.

Voice (Raj): "Some kanji were made in Japan. They're called kokuji, Japanese-created kanji."

## 3. 働 — THE PUSH THAT BECOMES WORK (0:09–0:22)

Visual: 動 sits on the left with two small arrows, ready to move. 人 walks in from the right and pushes it,
and the two meet. They lock into 働. A small ink figure pushes a load under it.

Voice (Raj): "This part is 動, ugoku, to move. Now add 人, hito, a person, who pushes it. Put them together,
and you get 働, hataraku, to work. A person moving something: that's work!"

On-screen: 動 うごく TO MOVE → 人 ひと PERSON → 働 はたらく TO WORK

## 4. 峠 — MOUNTAIN PASS (0:22–0:30)

Visual: 山 + 上 + 下, with a path climbing over the ridge and down the far side. The parts merge into 峠.

Voice (Raj): "Mountain, up, and down together make 峠, touge, a mountain pass."

## 5. 畑 — CULTIVATED FIELD (0:30–0:37)

Visual: 火 with a flame, plus 田 (rice field). They merge into 畑 over rows of crops.

Voice (Raj): "Fire, plus rice field, makes 畑, hatake, a cultivated field."

## 6. MORE KOKUJI (0:37–0:45)

Visual: eight cards laid down one by one: 凪 雫 辻 枠 匂 笹 榊 鰯, each with its reading and meaning.

Voice (Raj): "Over two hundred kokuji exist. Here are a few more!"

Pause on the cards so viewers can read them.

## 7. DREAM (0:45–0:58)

Visual: the kanji float up into the mist. A student walks toward the sunrise. 夢 is written in the sky.

Voice (Raj): "Every kanji has a story. Keep learning, one step at a time. Your Japanese journey is still being written!"

Japanese line (Raj in Japanese, on screen with romaji and English): 一歩ずつ、夢に近づこう。 (Ippo zutsu, yume ni chikazukō.) "Step by step, move closer to your dream."
