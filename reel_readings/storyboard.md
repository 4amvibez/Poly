### B. Every Japanese word is spoken by Raj in Japanese

In earlier reels, the English voice pronounced Japanese words badly (kōhī, "ha ha"). So in this reel, every Japanese word (やま, 富士山, さん, ひ, 日曜日, にち, みず, 水曜日, すい, ひと, 日本人, じん, 音読み, 訓読み) is a short clean clip in the **Raj in Japanese** voice. Each clip goes into a pause in the English sentence.

- The two voices never overlap; each Japanese word has a small breath on either side.
- Each clip is generated as a single word and checked with speech-to-text before use.
- Words that can be misread are given to the voice in kana, so it cannot guess the reading. For example, 日 is sent as ひ, and 日曜日 as にちようび.

Example: *"On its own, we say* **[やま]**. *But in* **[富士山]**, *we say* **[さん]**."
